04 · How we work

NINE STEPS,
ONE TEAM.

01

Vision & goals

A first conversation about the brand, the ambition, and the constraints. We listen more than we talk.

02

Research

Audience, category, competitors. Walk-arounds in the city. Quiet, careful field-work, not deck-padding.

03

Strategy

Positioning, narrative and the words to say it. We write it down so the team can use it on Monday.

04

Concept

Two or three opinionated directions. Each one is a real bet — no thin variations on the same idea.

05

Design system

Logo, type, colour, layout. Built once, durable for years. Tokens, components, the boring bits done right.

06

Product & spec

Tech-packs, material choices, dielines, prototypes. The bridge from the screen to the factory floor.

07

Sourcing

Mills, makers, factories. We open our network, negotiate the terms, and project-manage the build.

08

Production & QA

On-site checks, sample rounds, compliance. We don't ship anything we wouldn't put on our own shelf.

09

Launch & care

Final assets, campaign rails, store training. We stay on as a retained partner for the first season.
